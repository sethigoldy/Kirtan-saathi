# Privacy policy

Kirtan Saathi runs entirely on your computer. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

## Audio

Kirtan Saathi uses the microphone or mixer input only while **Start listening** is on. The audio is recognised on your computer, and Kirtan Saathi does not record it, keep it, or send it anywhere.

## What goes over the network

- **Update check.** Copies downloaded from GitHub ask GitHub (`api.github.com`) for the latest release shortly after starting and every 6 hours, when the computer is online. The request contains no personal data. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any web request. Turn this off under *App → Check for updates when online*. Copies from the Microsoft Store do not make this request: the Store updates them.
- **Screens and phones.** The projector, livestream overlay and phone pages are served by Kirtan Saathi on your computer. Other devices can reach them only when you turn on *Let phones on this Wi-Fi follow along*, and only from your local network. That traffic does not go to the internet.

## What is stored

Your settings (screen, look, language and update choices) are stored in `settings.json` in the app's data folder on your computer. They never leave it.
