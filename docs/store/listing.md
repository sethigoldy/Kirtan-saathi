## Description

Kirtan Saathi listens to the kirtan, paath or bani in the gurdwara and shows the matching Gurbani line on screen as it is sung or read. The sevadar presses Start listening; Kirtan Saathi finds the shabad and follows along. No typing, no searching.

• Kirtan: finds the shabad, then follows each line through repeats and back to the rahao.
• Paath: finds the place in a sehaj or akhand paath and follows the reading line by line, across shabads.
• Banis: follows Japji Sahib, Jaap Sahib, Rehras Sahib, Sohila, Anand Sahib, Sukhmani Sahib and others (29 banis).

Where the Gurbani is shown:
• Projector: full screen on a second screen, with themes, vishraam colours and adjustable text size.
• Livestream: a transparent lower third for OBS.
• Phones: the sangat follows the whole shabad on the gurdwara Wi-Fi, each in their own script and text size.

Under the tuk, if you choose: the tuk in Hindi script, the Punjabi arth (Prof. Sahib Singh and others) in Gurmukhi or Hindi script, and the English translation (Dr. Sant Singh Khalsa and others). Every source is credited on screen.

The whole app is available in Punjabi or English.

Kirtan Saathi works offline. Recognition runs on this computer; the audio is never recorded or sent anywhere. Only the Gurbani text itself is shown, never the recogniser's guesses.

## Features

- Finds the shabad from the kirtan, live
- Follows sehaj and akhand paath line by line
- Follows 29 banis, including Japji Sahib and Sukhmani Sahib
- Projector output with themes and vishraam colours
- OBS livestream overlay
- Phones on the gurdwara Wi-Fi follow along
- Hindi script, Punjabi arth and English translation
- Fully in Punjabi or English
- Works offline; the audio stays on the computer

## Restricted capability: runFullTrust (Submission options)

Kirtan Saathi is a desktop (Electron) app packaged with MSIX. It runs its speech recognition (ONNX Runtime) and the local presentation server for the projector and phones as a desktop process, which needs runFullTrust.

## Age ratings

Answer No throughout: Kirtan Saathi has no user-to-user communication, no user-generated content shared online, no purchases, no ads and no location sharing.
